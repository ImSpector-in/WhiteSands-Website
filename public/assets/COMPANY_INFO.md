# Company Information
History	    
White Sands Construction Inc. has built a broad base of experience in the General Construction Business. The company started in 1988 employing just a handful of employees. Currently White Sands Construction Inc. has a crew of twenty plus employees available to build your custom home or commercial project.

White Sands Construction Inc. is focused on continuing its growth through the reputation earned in the community. White Sands Construction will continue to focus on custom homes and commercial projects around the Islands.

White Sands Construction employs a compliment of in-house Painters, Heavy Equipment Operators and Concrete Finishers along with our full-time Carpenters (Journeymen and Apprentices) and Laborers. This helps to better control costs and craftmanship.
